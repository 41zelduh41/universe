The Universe

the universe slows her breath. it quickens again. she has to do this, wether she likes it or not. it was the same every single day. she wept in solitude. but, like a bolt of lightning, something... unreal, she felt. warmth. a butterfly. it flew towards her. she felt scared. she let it fly to her. it crawled inside her. to be with her. the butterfly didnt care about the scale of everything else. she was its. she was its universe. the universe felt that too. millions of billions of butterflies. this one is her's as she is its.
